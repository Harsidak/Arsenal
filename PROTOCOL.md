# Daily Protocol

> The goal is not perfection. The goal is maximum iterations per day.
> Make mistakes fast. Log them. Extract the pattern. Move on.

---

## 🔴 Every Day (Non-Negotiable)

1. **Math** (Axler or Joseph) — Do at least 1 exercise. Write the proof/derivation. If stuck >15 min, log in `MISTAKES.md` and move on.
2. **Code** — Solve 1 CP problem (C++) OR implement 1 concept from scratch (Python/PyTorch). Ship something.
3. **Log** — At least 1 entry in `MISTAKES.md` before bed. If you didn't make a mistake, you weren't pushing hard enough.

## 🟡 Weekly

- **Pattern Review** (Sunday) — Re-read `MISTAKES.md`. Pull any recurring patterns into the Extracted Patterns section.
- **Deep Implementation** (any day) — One longer session: implement a paper section, build a training pipeline, or grind a hard CP problem set.

## 🟢 Monthly

- **Concept Audit** — Pick 3 concepts you studied last month. Can you explain them from scratch without notes? If not, re-derive.
- **Mistake Audit** — Count your Extracted Patterns. Are they shrinking? Are new categories appearing?
