What actually happens in an interview:

1) 3-4 people from YC partners

2) They will be basic questions. They are just trying to understand the fundamentals of the business. This includes questions about how you plan to acquire users and scale the business.

3) One thing they consider is whether there are positive indications that a productive working relationship can be established. This process includes understanding what factors influence your collaboration with YC partners.

4) They are looking for trustworthiness.

5) Interviews are going to get fast and intense; they are going to get as much information as possible. Demonstrating mastery in business, understanding the numbers, identifying the most significant problems you face, and presenting clear ideas for what to work on next is essential.

6) They are looking for things that they may not have understood from the application in the first place.

7) A successful interview is not about memorizing or saying the correct answers to a question; it is about demonstrating effective communication and clarity of thought.

8) They expect significant risks in early-stage startups. Self-awareness about the risks is beneficial.
