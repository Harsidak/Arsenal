### Video 2: Startup School — *"The Sales Playbook For Founders"*
- **Source:** [YouTube Video (`DH7REvnQ1y4`)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DH7REvnQ1y4) & [YC Startup Library](https://www.ycombinator.com/library/Mo-the-sales-playbook-for-founders)
- **Core Lessons & Verbatim Quotes:**
  1. **The #1 B2B Trap: Unpaid, Unbounded "Design Partnerships":**
     > *"90% of the time, the vast majority of the time, founders get stuck in the very early stages—it's the very long unpaid design partnerships... Customers treat founders in a design partnership as like an unpaid dev shop... You've got this fancy logo on your website as a design partner which feels like progress to you, and you're pretty proud of it, so you don't want to remove it from your website, but really you're not getting any closer to real revenue."* ([YouTube `DH7REvnQ1y4` at 00:59 & 02:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DH7REvnQ1y4))
  2. **The 48-Hour Narrow Wedge & Going Undercover:**
     > *"We've seen some of the best founders even go undercover and get qualified to work as an auditor or a real estate agent or an accountant and actually go and do the work—like get a job for a couple of months so they deeply understand the problem in the domain space. Really the goal of all of this is to identify a really narrow burning problem, and you'd be able to go away and build a narrow wedge product in as little as 48 hours and bring it back to the customer."* ([YouTube `DH7REvnQ1y4` at 03:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DH7REvnQ1y4))
  3. **Track "Time to First Value" Like a North Star (Do Janky Stuff, Skip API Integrations in Pilots):**
     > *"I'd track the time to first value like a north star metric—reducing it from weeks to hours is often the biggest single lever for higher pilot-to-paid conversion. What this actually means is doing janky stuff to get your product live as soon as possible. For example, I would never do a full API integration during a pilot if it requires any engineering time from the customer whatsoever, because that's just going to delay you by months. If you can work from Excel imports and exports, or ask the customer to email you the data you need and then email back the completed work... you're really trying to get to the point where they can experience the value of your product as quickly as possible."* ([YouTube `DH7REvnQ1y4` at 12:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DH7REvnQ1y4))
  4. **The Pro Move: Annual/Monthly Recurring Contracts with a 30–60 Day Opt-Out:**
     > *"To get around [negotiating a second time after a paid pilot], really sophisticated founders move on from paid pilots to recurring revenue contracts with an opt-out period. So this is typically a monthly or annually recurring contract with a 30- or 60-day money-back guarantee or opt-out period at the very start. By default, if the customer does nothing and is happy with your product, it's going to turn into a full recurring contract after that opt-out period with no additional sales process needed. That's the pro move—it's like magic."* ([YouTube `DH7REvnQ1y4` at 13:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DH7REvnQ1y4))

### Video 3: Startup School — *"How To Price For B2B"*
- **Source:** [YouTube Video (`4hjiRmgmHiU`)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hjiRmgmHiU) & [YC Startup Library](https://www.ycombinator.com/library/LI-how-to-price-for-b2b)
- **Core Pricing Rules:**
  - **The Value Equation (Charge ~1/3 of Delivered Value):** Sit down with your internal champion, calculate the exact dollar value saved or generated (e.g., 100 support agents × $100k fully loaded cost = $10M; automating 20% saves $2M/year), and **charge 25–50% (~1/3) of that value** ($700k/year so the customer keeps $1.3M) ([YouTube `4hjiRmgmHiU` at 01:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hjiRmgmHiU)).
  - **Never Price Cost-Plus (Target 80–90% Gross Margin, Treat Free AI Credits as Cash Cost):** Cost is only a floor; if 1/3 of your value equation ($150k) is less than your OpenAI/AWS cost ($200k), you are in a bad business. Never hide behind free OpenAI/AWS startup credits ([YouTube `4hjiRmgmHiU` at 04:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hjiRmgmHiU)).
  - **The +50% Price Escalation Heuristic:** If you don't know what to charge, pick a comparable price ($10k) and **increase your price by 50% every time you pitch a new customer** ($10k → $15k → $22k) until you lose >25% of deals solely on price ([YouTube `4hjiRmgmHiU` at 15:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hjiRmgmHiU)).
  - **The 5:1 Sales Rep Quota Rule:** An Account Executive must close **5x their total annual compensation** in new ARR (e.g., a $100k OTE rep must close $500k new ARR/year, which works for 10 × $50k whales or 20 × $25k deals, but fails for $1,000/yr SMB deals) ([YouTube `4hjiRmgmHiU` at 11:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hjiRmgmHiU)).

### Videos 4 & 5: Startup School — *"B2B Startup Metrics"* (`_mKeVGSqQac`) & *"Consumer Startup Metrics"* (`fdD4y4Civp4`)
- **Source:** [B2B Startup Metrics (`_mKeVGSqQac`)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_mKeVGSqQac) & [Consumer Startup Metrics (`fdD4y4Civp4`)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fdD4y4Civp4)
- **Tom's Quantitative Benchmarks (What He Will Grill You On in the Interview):**
  - **The Top 3 Metrics Every Founder Must Know Cold:** **Revenue** (never GMV or GTV—Tom recounts a Middle Eastern neobank bragging about 50% biweekly GTV growth while revenue was flat for two months because of cashback rebates), **Net Burn Rate**, and **Runway** ([YouTube `_mKeVGSqQac` at 06:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_mKeVGSqQac)).
  - **B2B Net Dollar Retention (NDR):** Must be **125%–150%+ for early-stage B2B SaaS** (and 110%–120% at maturity). If NDR is <100%, you have a leaky bucket and must stop spending on sales/marketing until you fix the product ([YouTube `_mKeVGSqQac` at 15:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_mKeVGSqQac)).
  - **Consumer Growth Rate:** **15% month-over-month** active user growth is the target (5x/year); 10% MoM is okay (3x/year); **≤5% MoM will not reach breakout scale** ([YouTube `fdD4y4Civp4` at 01:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fdD4y4Civp4)).
  - **Consumer Organic vs. Paid Split:** Must be **80%–100% organic** (driven by viral loops and multiplayer network effects like Monzo's bill-splitting pots and Golden Tickets); anything **below 50% organic** is a major red flag because Google and Meta ad auctions bid your margins to zero ([YouTube `fdD4y4Civp4` at 09:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fdD4y4Civp4)).
  - **Consumer Net Promoter Score (NPS) & "Magic Moment":** Minimum **+50 NPS** for a new consumer startup (Monzo was **+75 to +80**, Tesla is +96). Identify the **"Magic Moment"** that predicts long-term retention (at Monzo, users who added **3 friends** from their phonebook in the first few days retained **20 percentage points higher**) ([YouTube `fdD4y4Civp4` at 15:35 & 18:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fdD4y4Civp4)).
  - **Never Scale Negative Unit Economics:** At Monzo, they lost £30–£40 per customer across their first 500,000 users before bringing tech in-house and flipping to +£30–£40 per customer. In today's interest-rate environment, fix unit economics *before* scaling ([YouTube `_mKeVGSqQac` at 21:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_mKeVGSqQac)).

## 4. How Tom Blomfield Evaluates Startups in the 20-Minute YC Zoom Interview

### The Famous Max Junestrand ([Legora](https://finance.biggo.com/news/742130fbc4ba300c)) Interview Story: What Happens If You Don't Know Your Customer Segment
In May 2023, Max Junestrand (co-founder & CEO of **Legora**, which later grew from $1M to $100M ARR in 20 months) walked into his first YC interview with Tom Blomfield ([BigGo News](https://finance.biggo.com/news/742130fbc4ba300c)).
- **Tom's Question:** *"What type of lawyers are you serving?"*
- **Max's Answer:** *"What do you mean, are there different types of lawyers?"*
- **Tom's Reaction:** Tom laughed out loud during the interview, and rejected them on that pass—only accepting them two months later after the founders went back, obsessed over legal workflows, and proved they could learn faster than anyone else ([BigGo News](https://finance.biggo.com/news/742130fbc4ba300c)).
- **The Takeaway:** Tom will test your **granular domain specificity** in the first 3 minutes. If you say *"We sell to lawyers"*, *"We sell to banks"*, or *"We sell to construction companies"* without knowing the exact sub-vertical, buyer persona, software stack (e.g., Procore, Salesforce, MS Word), and unit value equation, he will expose it immediately.

### What Tom Blomfield Looks For (The 6 Core Green Flags)
1. **Teach Him Something New in 3–4 Minutes:** Show deep, obsessive domain mastery—ideally from living inside the customer's workplace (like [Toma's](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/toma) founders spending 6 months coding inside hot car dealership service bays, or going undercover as an auditor/agent) ([20VC Interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TyeesSulYY0), [Sales Playbook](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DH7REvnQ1y4)).
2. **Non-Linear "System Hacker" Exceptionalism:** Evidence in your background that you hacked a real-world system, built something from scratch early in life, or achieved top-0.1% outlier results outside the standard "prestigious university → consulting/banking conveyor belt" ([20VC Interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TyeesSulYY0)).
3. **Simultaneous Mastery of the "1% Billion-Dollar Vision" and "This Week's Narrow Wedge":** State clearly how your company becomes a multi-billion-dollar category king *and* why your current product is a hyper-narrow, high-polish wedge that delivers value in hours without customer engineering effort ([20VC Interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TyeesSulYY0), [Sales Playbook](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DH7REvnQ1y4)).
4. **Radical Honesty & Command of Real Metrics:** Know your exact **Revenue (ARR/MRR)**, **Net Burn**, **Runway**, **Net Dollar Retention**, **Gross Margin (including unsubsidized LLM token costs)**, and **Organic vs. Paid CAC** without hiding behind GMV, GTV, or unpaid design-partner logos ([B2B Startup Metrics](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_mKeVGSqQac)).
5. **Tight B2B Sales Velocity (Opt-Out Contracts Over Endless Free Pilots):** Show that you ask for money upfront, define a clear **Value Equation** (charging ~1/3 of ROI), and convert customers via short paid pilots or annual contracts with a 30–60 day opt-out ([Sales Playbook](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DH7REvnQ1y4), [How To Price For B2B](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hjiRmgmHiU)).
6. **AI-Native Leverage ("Burn Tokens, Not Headcount"):** Show how a 2–4 person engineering team is shipping at the speed of a 40-person team using coding agents and recursive self-improving loops ([Self-Improving Company Talk](https://www.ycombinator.com/library/Qf-how-to-build-a-self-improving-company-with-ai)).

### Red Flags That Will Lose Tom Blomfield
- **Co-Founders Contradicting Each Other on the Call:** Tom still remembers cringing through his own S11 interview when he, Hiroki, and Matt contradicted each other on every question because all three wanted to be CEO ([20VC Interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TyeesSulYY0)). Have clear roles and never talk over or contradict your co-founder.
- **Bragging About Unpaid 6-Month "Design Partnerships" or "Pre-POCs":** Tom views long, unpaid design partnerships as founders being used as an *"unpaid dev shop"* because they are *"too meek to ask for money"* ([Sales Playbook](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DH7REvnQ1y4)).
- **Reporting GTV / GMV / Pageviews to Hide Flat Revenue or Negative Margins:** He will immediately ask what your net revenue and gross margin are after deducting cash token/infrastructure costs ([B2B Startup Metrics](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_mKeVGSqQac)).
- **Broad, Mediocre Feature Creep Instead of a Narrow, Polished Wedge:** Trying to clone all of Google Docs or an entire ERP at mediocre quality instead of nailing one burning pain point in 48 hours ([20VC Interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TyeesSulYY0), [Sales Playbook](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DH7REvnQ1y4)).

## 6. Tactical Playbook: How to Win Tom Blomfield in 20 Minutes

1. **Open With a Surprising, Ground-Level Industry Insight (The "3-Minute Teach Test"):**
   - Don't give generic TAM slides. Tell him something counterintuitive that you only discovered by sitting next to your customer's keyboard, living in their workplace, or doing their job manually ([20VC Interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TyeesSulYY0), [Sales Playbook](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DH7REvnQ1y4)).
2. **Present Your Exact "Value Equation" and "Opt-Out Contract" Motion:**
   - If you are B2B, state clearly: *"Our customer spends $X on this problem today. Our narrow wedge saves them $Y in hours/cost within 48 hours of onboarding via simple CSV/email without any API engineering. We charge 1/3 of that value ($Z/year) on an annual contract with a 30-day opt-out."* ([Sales Playbook](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DH7REvnQ1y4), [How To Price For B2B](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hjiRmgmHiU)).
3. **If You Are Consumer, Lead With Organic Virality, Magic Moment & NPS:**
   - Show how your product turns a single-player utility into a **multiplayer network effect** or high-status scarcity loop (like Monzo's Golden Tickets and group holiday pots), state your month-over-month organic growth rate (aiming for **15%+ MoM** and **>80% organic**), and name the exact **"Magic Moment"** behavior that locks in retention ([Consumer Startup Metrics](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fdD4y4Civp4)).
4. **Never Bluff on Metrics or Hide Behind GTV/Unpaid Logos:**
   - If your revenue is $0, say "$0" up front and explain the exact experiments you ran over the last 2 weeks and why they failed—remember, Tom's favorite founder sent 10 consecutive monthly investor updates with a giant **$0** at the top before breaking out ([B2B Startup Metrics](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_mKeVGSqQac)).
5. **Show How You "Burn Tokens, Not Headcount":**
   - Demonstrate how your team uses AI coding agents and recursive self-improving loops (capturing customer errors/feedback and shipping fixes overnight) to operate at 10x the speed of legacy incumbents ([Self-Improving Company Talk](https://www.ycombinator.com/library/Qf-how-to-build-a-self-improving-company-with-ai)).
