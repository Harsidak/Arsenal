## 2. Insights from YouTube Videos, Talks & Podcasts ("Watched" & Analyzed)

Because Grey Baker is a hands-on product builder rather than a traditional VC pundit, his public talks and podcast appearances reveal the exact operational scar tissue he brings into a 20-minute YC interview. Below is the synthesis of every major video and audio appearance featuring Grey Baker.

### Video 1: Indie Bites Ep. 60 — *"Growing Dependabot to $14k MRR before selling to Github - Grey Baker, Dependabot"*
- **Source:** [YouTube Video (`zVVDKpElpP4`)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zVVDKpElpP4) & [Indie Bites Show Notes](https://indiebites.com/60)
- **Core Lessons & Verbatim Quotes:**
  1. **Why Launch-Day Hype & Content Marketing Fail Without Direct Sales:**
     - When launching Dependabot, Grey spent two days analyzing 10 years of open-source vulnerabilities to craft a data-heavy, clickbaity Hacker News launch post:
     > *"We were like, 'Okay, well we built this great thing, everyone's gonna want it, they're like me, they'll just see the value. We'll just do like a marketing launch, we'll write a great blog post and we'll put it all over Hacker News and job done...' And it came to launch day and we put it live and it absolutely tanked, and we had one sign-up by the end of the day. And man, that is really depressing."* ([YouTube `zVVDKpElpP4` at 10:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zVVDKpElpP4))
  2. **Why Eating Your Own Dogfood Saves You from Quitting Too Early:**
     > *"Because we were using it ourselves, we didn't give up because we knew it was valuable. It didn't seem like a lack of validation of the idea—you know, it felt like we were just crap at marketing."* ([YouTube `zVVDKpElpP4` at 11:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zVVDKpElpP4))
  3. **Manual Concierge Outreach ("Spamming Pull Requests") & Learning from Rejections:**
     - Instead of writing more blog posts, Grey searched GitHub every day for developers who had manually submitted dependency update PRs—the exact task Dependabot automated—and commented directly on their PRs:
     > *"What I then started doing is spamming people on GitHub who had done something that the robot could have done for them. And I would go on and find their pull requests and write a comment saying, 'Hey, I wrote this robot and it could do this for you and it's great and you should use it, please use it, it's free.' And that worked quite well. I couldn't find that many of those people each day, but the ones that I could find, like 50% of them signed up. I mean, it's the 'do things that don't scale' mantra. In those really early days, doing that like concierge sales—even if you are literally giving away your thing for free—is so valuable, because even the people that didn't sign up told me why... They said, 'Oh, I'd love to use this, but I'm worried about these things.' And that's what I used to make the product better. I did that for about two months."* ([YouTube `zVVDKpElpP4` at 11:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zVVDKpElpP4))
  4. **Embedded Distribution Channels (The GitHub Marketplace Inflection Point):**
     > *"The big change for Dependabot was we got into this thing called the GitHub Marketplace, and they would take the payment on our behalf and pay it out to us, and they'd also kind of advertise us within their platform. Overnight, our distribution problem wasn't solved, but was dramatically improved, because then we were getting 20 sign-ups a day from there."* ([YouTube `zVVDKpElpP4` at 12:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zVVDKpElpP4))
  5. **Retention & Customer Advocacy Over Paid Acquisition (The GoCardless & Dependabot Law):**
     > *"My philosophy has always been to put pleasing existing customers first—make sure that we're retaining those customers, make sure we're turning those customers into advocates, and then with any extra time I have, that's when I think about, 'Okay, how can we distribute to others?'... We're really word-of-mouth driven, and that works in developer tools. Word of mouth is pretty powerful and developers are very skeptical, so I haven't found other channels that have great returns."* ([YouTube `zVVDKpElpP4` at 12:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zVVDKpElpP4))
  6. **The "Petrol-Soaked Snickers Bar" Story (Founder Grit Under Scarcity):**
     - While cycling through Uzbekistan with no cash, half a day of food, and 240 miles to the nearest town with a bank, Grey rationed himself to one Snickers bar every 100 miles—only to discover a leaking lighter had dissolved the wrapper glue and chocolate in his waterproof pannier. He ate the lighter-fluid-soaked Snickers bar with a spork anyway because starving 240 miles from a bank was worse ([YouTube `zVVDKpElpP4` at 07:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zVVDKpElpP4)).
  7. **Start With Sales, Not Marketing — And Ignore Vanity Metrics:**
     > *"Don't compare yourself to other startups when you hear somebody else got a thousand sign-ups on Product Hunt... You win by focusing on your own thing and by keeping going. Don't give up. Start with sales, not marketing. This stuff takes ages and ages. The companies that from the outside look like they're winning, on the inside feel like they're losing. That's how you will feel every day."* ([YouTube `zVVDKpElpP4` at 13:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zVVDKpElpP4))

### Video 2: TFiR Secure IT — *"GitHub Is Baking Security Into Developer Workflow"*
- **Source:** [YouTube Video (`orjJPZ0_ERM`)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orjJPZ0_ERM)
- **Core Lessons & Verbatim Quotes:**
  1. **Why Standalone Tools Fail & Embedded Workflow Tools Win:**
     - Grey explains why legacy enterprise software gets ignored: when a tool sits in a separate dashboard for a separate team, developers ignore it.
     > *"Security researchers are outnumbered by developers by about 500 to 1. So if you want to talk about embedding security in the developer workflow, you really need a solution that's going to be automated and that's going to scale... GitHub comes at this—we are really a developer-first company from the outset. What we build is the developer tool that developers live in, so we're always thinking about how do you embed new things into that flow."* ([YouTube `orjJPZ0_ERM` at 01:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orjJPZ0_ERM))
  2. **The 2x Actionability Rule (Why Alerts Fail and Pull Requests Win):**
     - When asked why engineers ignore security alerts even when patches exist, Grey gives the single most important product insight behind both **Dependabot** and **Pincites**:
     > *"The reason that we don't apply those patches as engineers is because it feels like tedious work and we've got other priorities. And so if we can make that easy enough, it makes a huge difference. We've seen at GitHub, we've sent about a million of these automated pull requests to fix vulnerabilities. The fix rate for those vulnerabilities if you get a pull request versus if you just get an alert—it doubles. So making it easy to do the right thing, that's huge."* ([YouTube `orjJPZ0_ERM` at 12:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orjJPZ0_ERM))
     - **Notice the exact pattern across Grey's entire career:**
       - **Dependabot:** Didn't build a separate vulnerability dashboard; opened a ready-to-merge **GitHub Pull Request** directly in the repo ([YouTube `orjJPZ0_ERM`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orjJPZ0_ERM)).
       - **GitHub Code Scanning:** Didn't build a separate SAST portal for security teams; surfaced CodeQL checks as inline **Pull Request reviews** with a <30% false-positive rate ([GitHub Satellite 2020](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=58N0_0HCDPE)).
       - **Pincites (YC S23):** Didn't ask lawyers to leave their document editor for a separate CLM portal; embedded AI playbook redlining directly inside **Microsoft Word** ([Startups.RIP](https://startups.rip/company/pincites)).

## 3. How Grey Baker Evaluates Startups in the 20-Minute YC Zoom Interview

When you sit across from Grey Baker on Zoom—whether in a standard batch interview or a buildathon fast-track like the [Bolna Symphony Buildathon YC Interview](https://www.bolna.ai/symphony/buildathon)—you are talking to someone who has **written the code himself, done manual outbound sales on GitHub PRs, navigated FCA financial regulation, scaled an enterprise business unit to $140M ARR, and sold two startups** ([YesPress](https://yespress.io/grey-baker), [The CEO Library](https://theceolibrary.com/grey-baker-9256.html)).

### What Grey Baker Looks For (The 6 Core Green Flags)

1. **"Embedded in the Existing Workflow" vs. "Yet Another Dashboard":**
   - Across Dependabot (PRs inside GitHub), Pincites (redlining inside Microsoft Word), and his YC portfolio ([Agent Relay](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/agent-relay) inside Claude Code/Cursor/Codex, [Horizontal](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/horizontal) and [Lark](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/lark-2) inside existing factory/distributor ERPs, [Shire Intelligence](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/shire-intelligence) running on existing restaurant CCTV cameras), Grey is obsessed with **zero-friction adoption** ([YouTube `orjJPZ0_ERM`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orjJPZ0_ERM)).
   - **What he wants to hear:** *"We don't ask users to rip and replace their ERP or log into a new portal. Our agent lives directly inside the workflow they already open 50 times a day, and Instead of giving them an alert, we hand them the completed action/patch ready to approve in one click."*
2. **Concierge Sales Before Marketing ("Spamming PRs" Hustle):**
   - Having watched his own 2-day Hacker News data post get **1 sign-up** before getting **50% conversion** by manually commenting on individual GitHub pull requests ([YouTube `zVVDKpElpP4`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zVVDKpElpP4)), Grey immediately probes how you got (or plan to get) your first 10 customers.
   - **What he wants to hear:** How you identified the exact moment a target user experiences pain, reached out to them 1-on-1, watched them use your product, and cataloged the exact objections of the 50% who said no to fix the product.
3. **Deterministic Reliability & Verification Underneath AI Agents:**
   - Having integrated Semmle/CodeQL and built enterprise security policy-as-code at GitHub ([YouTube `58N0_0HCDPE`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=58N0_0HCDPE)), Grey knows enterprise buyers will not give an AI agent write access if it hallucinates or floods production.
   - Look at the companies he chose to mentor in Fall 2026:
     - **[Rystic (YC F26)](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/rystic):** Stateful local binary simulators of Stripe, Slack, and Google Drive verified daily against 1,813 real API checks (99.1% match) because an AI agent at the founders' previous job flooded CI runners with hundreds of GitHub PRs.
     - **[Altair Health (YC F26)](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/altair-health):** Formalized health insurers' billing policies and placed a **mathematical theorem prover** underneath the AI billing agent so no invalid claim action ever executes without human biller review.
   - **What he wants to hear:** How you bound, verify, or prove the reliability and auditability of your system in production when mistakes cost real money.
4. **First-Principles Problem Selection (Solving "Boring, Tedious" Bottlenecks):**
   - Grey describes dependency updates as *"a really boring problem, quite a small problem, but as a result the perfect thing to build on"* ([YouTube `zVVDKpElpP4`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zVVDKpElpP4)), and praises Taiichi Ohno's *Toyota Production System* for building an *"information nervous system"* on the factory floor ([The CEO Library](https://theceolibrary.com/grey-baker-9256.html)).
   - He backs founders turning messy, manual human coordination into an automated "information nervous system"—whether in factories ([Horizontal](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/horizontal)), wholesale distributors ([Lark](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/lark-2)), public-company Investor Relations ([Collar](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/collar)), restaurant floors ([Shire Intelligence](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/shire-intelligence)), or Global South tourist visas ([Veeza AI](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/veeza-ai)).
5. **High-Velocity, Self-Sufficient Technical Builders (Especially Outlier Credibility):**
   - Grey taught himself to code in a library in 6 months after winning Cambridge's top economics prize ([YesPress](https://yespress.io/grey-baker)). He loves founders with extreme technical horsepower and unconventional proof of excellence—such as [Rystic's](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/rystic) pro *Magic: The Gathering* champions who built Roblox's service mesh, [Nitroduck's](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/nitroduck) iGEM World Grand Prize winner & National Decathlon Champion, [Petrichor's](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/petrichor) 19-year-old wet-lab builders, and [Streamline Systems'](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/streamline-systems) Cambridge aerospace engineers.
6. **Capital Efficiency & Unit-Economics Sanity:**
   - Because Grey killed his own first startup idea when he realized the capital-investment math didn't work and bootstrapped Dependabot to $14k MRR on $0 of VC ([Indie Bites Transcript](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zVVDKpElpP4)), he spots capital-inefficient business models or fake top-line vanity metrics immediately.

### Red Flags That Will Lose Grey Baker in a 20-Minute Call
- **Jargon Parrot Syndrome:** Using buzzwords ("kanban", "DevSecOps", "agentic swarm") without understanding the underlying mechanics from first principles ([The CEO Library](https://theceolibrary.com/grey-baker-9256.html)).
- **"We Need Marketing / Paid Ads to Validate Demand":** Claiming you haven't acquired users yet because you need funding for marketing, PR, or conferences instead of doing manual 1-on-1 outbound sales ([YouTube `zVVDKpElpP4`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zVVDKpElpP4)).
- **High-Friction / Noisy Product UX:** Building a tool that floods users with false positives (>30% noise) or forces users to copy-paste between windows instead of completing the action inline ([YouTube `58N0_0HCDPE`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=58N0_0HCDPE), [YouTube `orjJPZ0_ERM`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orjJPZ0_ERM)).

### Questions to Expect from Grey Baker in Your YC Interview
1. *"Walk me through the exact workflow of your user right now. Where do they click, what system do they live in all day, and why won't your product just become another tab they ignore?"*
2. *"How did you get your current users? Not your launch post—how did you find them one by one, and what did the people who refused to sign up tell you they were worried about?"*
3. *"What is your false-positive or failure rate right now? When your system/agent gets something wrong in production, what happens, and how does the user audit or override it?"*
4. *"Why haven't the incumbents or existing teams automated this already? What changed technically that makes this solvable today?"*
5. *"What is the single most tedious manual step your customer still has to do even after they install your product, and how do you eliminate that so doing the right thing takes one click?"*

## 5. Tactical Playbook: How to Win Grey Baker in 20 Minutes

If Grey Baker is on your YC interview panel, execute these five rules:

1. **Lead With the Workflow Integration, Not the Model Architecture:**
   - Show in the first 30 seconds *where* your product sits in the user's daily habit loop and how it turns a multi-step chore into a **1-click approval or automated pull request** ([YouTube `orjJPZ0_ERM`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orjJPZ0_ERM)).
2. **Demonstrate "Concierge Sales" Scars:**
   - Quantify your manual outreach: how many targeted prospects you messaged, what percentage converted, and—most importantly—the exact product changes you shipped based on the objections of the people who said *no* ([YouTube `zVVDKpElpP4`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zVVDKpElpP4)).
3. **Show Your Verification / Guardrail Layer:**
   - If you are building with AI or automation in a high-stakes domain (code, finance, healthcare, industrial ops, legal), explicitly explain how you prevent false positives, hallucinations, or runaway actions (e.g., deterministic checks, simulators, audit logs, human-in-the-loop thresholds) ([YouTube `58N0_0HCDPE`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=58N0_0HCDPE), [Rystic](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/rystic), [Altair Health](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/altair-health)).
4. **Speak from First Principles, Never Buzzwords:**
   - Grey is a Cambridge mathematician and economist who loves Taiichi Ohno's factory-floor logic and mocks people who parrot management buzzwords like "kanban" without knowing their origin ([The CEO Library](https://theceolibrary.com/grey-baker-9256.html)). Explain your unit economics, customer bottleneck, and technical architecture in plain, concrete terms.
5. **Prove Retention & Advocacy Over Top-of-Funnel Hype:**
   - Highlight users who use your product daily and refer peers organically (like [Veeza AI's](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/veeza-ai) multi-generational family referrals or [Lark's](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/lark-2) 50 hours/week saved per customer) rather than launch-day pageviews ([YouTube `zVVDKpElpP4`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zVVDKpElpP4)).
