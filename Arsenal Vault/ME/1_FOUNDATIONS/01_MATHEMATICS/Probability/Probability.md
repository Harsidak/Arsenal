# Probability

Probability is the mathematical language of uncertainty, providing a rigorous calculus for modeling incomplete information, stochastic systems, and inference in [[MATHEMATICS]] and [[BOOK - MATHEMATICS FOR MACHINE LEARNING (Deisenroth)]].

---

## 🗺️ Probability Map of Content (MOC)

### 1. Foundations & Axiomatic Structure
*   [[Sample Space and Events]]: Definitions of $\Omega$, $\sigma$-algebras, and events as subsets.
*   [[Naive Probability and Combinatorics]]: Counting principles, permutations, combinations, and story proofs.
*   [[Kolmogorov Axioms and Probability Spaces]]: The formal triple $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, P)$ and core theorems.

### 2. Conditioning & Independence
*   [[Conditional Probability and Bayes Rule]]: Updating belief under evidence; law of total probability.
*   [[Independence and Conditional Independence]]: Statistical orthogonality and graphical model foundations.

### 3. Random Variables & Distributions
*   [[Random Variables and Distributions]]: Definition of RV as a measurable function $X: \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$, Support, and Image.
*   [[Probability Mass Functions (PMF)]]: Discrete distributions, valid PMF axioms, and Dirac-style discrete weighting.
*   [[Cumulative Distribution Functions (CDF)]]: Right-continuity, non-decreasing limits, and unified bridge to continuous variables.
*   [[Named Discrete Distributions]]: Bernoulli, Binomial, Hypergeometric, Geometric, Negative Binomial, Poisson.
