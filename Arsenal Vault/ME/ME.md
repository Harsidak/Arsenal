1) Believe in god firmly: having full faith god is a must he is the only one true one in this world. He is present everywhere I must see him in everyone in everyplace and everywhere. There are some things in my control and some aren't and both of them are given by him. my body, my soul, my brain, intellect is given by him and in my control. my parents and others things aren't in my control and still given by him. 

2) So this is just a simulation where we have to act and play this role that god has given to me nothing else. There is nothing mine. when there is nothing mine then there is nothing to win and nothing to lose in this world. 

3) I must perish some day this body cannot hold this soul for long as it belongs to him. Evaluation must be done for this soul over once it reaches it sanctuary.

4) this soul (Mann)'s house is akaal (the timeless) so this soul is a small part of him. If I actually trust him then in that case I must not generate any self doubt, disbelief, and under confidence and low thinking as I am the part of the greater akaal it self. I must never let my moral down under any circumstances.

5) The body must stay in its house (with worldly family) and the Mann must stay in its house (Akaal) and doing this while acting and playing our role the who is able to live household living and still be detached from the world at the same time is one true one. 

6) "esi bast koi nahi, jo mein khalse deho nahi" ~ there is nothing as such thing which I (god) has not given to khalsa. by the definition khalsa is someone who follows the rehatnam (basic religious laws) loves god at all times and becomes his tool or vessel completely. 

7) One must realize that he is not the doer the doer is Akaal but the he/her is the doing the work flows from him(god) through him (individual person).

8) "Khalsa mero roop he khass" ~ Khalsa is my (God's)special or extraordinary form. 

9) A person is able to achieve all the characteristics of khalsa: fearless, most elegant, etc.  or become khalsa if the soul and body are combined. 10th guru has given the soul (jot) to guru granth sahib ji Maharaj and the body (the vessel) to his 5th son (the khalsa) combines the soul(teachings of guru, characteristics, understandings and sayings) combined all together with the body (Discipline, wisdom intellect, politics) a person is able to achieve his true form of what he is suppose to become on this earth.

10) The power of true khalsa is so enormously elegant and elevated that he/she can eat 1 million people alive in breakfast. respect, care and love for those in need and slaughter and cut those down who oppress others. He is so handsome, tall and his beauty or state of mind cannot be described. Using his intellectual wisdom he build the nations, systems and empires with the help of scholar warriors robust enough to withstand every wave coming in there way. His actions in this world are perfect and he finally would descend to the sanctuary of god(Sachkhand). He alone has the enough power to realise this simulation (Maya) in which he is living in. He alone has the power to realize that this world is like a dream and he is just here to act and play his role.
